About Me

I am Abdulhamid Ansari, a faculty member in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Hormozgan with over 16 years of combined academic and industrial experience. My work centers on advanced modeling, simulation, and optimization of thermofluidic systems, with a particular focus on steam power plants and solar energy.

I develop high‑fidelity simulations using Modelica, Mathematica, Python, Julia (CUDA), EES, Aspen HYSYS, Ansys Fluent, and COMSOL. I authored the Persian book “Introduction to Modeling of Engineering Systems by Modelica”, published several journal papers, and created two free EES training courses. In parallel, I have built an Android thermodynamics calculator that merges scientific computing with comprehensive property databases.

Research Interests: Practical dynamic modeling & optimization • Data‑driven ML modeling • Interdisciplinary thermofluidic systems • Programming & tools development • Engineering education

Thermodynamics Calculator App

📱 Scientific Calculator + Thermodynamic Property Solver

A professional Android app that integrates a full scientific calculator with extensive thermodynamic databases (steam, refrigerants, humid air, etc.). Perform state‑point calculations, cycle analysis, and unit conversions anywhere.
